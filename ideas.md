### Situations
- Gain Boons From Condition Completion
  - Survivors gain rewards for completing tasks
  - Ominous/Trial vaults as native currency
  - Special items that harass the Ghost
    - Crucifix
      - Using it reveals the Ghost within a radius
      - Can slow it and make it noisy too
      - Prevent boosting or teleporting
      - Has a max use time
    - Radar
      - Dot for the Ghost's direction appears around you, but not super accurate
    - Shoddy Compass
      - Breaks after some amount of updates
    - Spear with limited durability
    - Trident with limited durability
      - Loyalty?
    - Item that grants abilty to fly for a second or two
      - Could come with rockets, or that could be another item
    - Item Stocking
      - Vending machines accept money in exchange for items that anybody can use
      - Enough conditions met grants everybody a one-time use
        - Turn on generator
        - Fix supply lines
        - Turn on local network
    - Potions
    - Can summon and lead Allays for some reason
- Invincible Ghost
  - When the ghost dies, it has ~15-30 seconds before respawning
  - It could be much weaker allowing death to occur often but survivor resources dwindle
  - Survivors must complete some tasks to allow the cycle to end
- Timer Changes
  - Timer doesn't fully deplete until conditions met
    - These conditions can be iterative, so each condition allows the timer to dwindle a little further
  - The timer is flipped - surviors must beat the clock to complete the tasks before the timer switches
    - The timer can either stop, or maybe even flip so that if it ends, the ghost loses
      - It can flip two ways
        - Inverts, so the ghost has the remaining time to hunt the survivors before it loses
        - Reverses, so if you took 3 minutes to complete, the ghost now has 3 minutes left
  - The timer isn't a win condition
    - The lower the timer, the harder the ghost
    - The conditions add time, to keep the ghost manageable
- Conditions prevent a third party from interfering
  - If the task isn't completed by a certain time, waves of zombies or other enemies flood the area
  - Doesn't necessarily have to make it a guaranteed victory, but it can greatly hinder the survivors
- Completing conditions opens shortcuts or other pathways
  - This might be achievable entirely through redstone with resetting markers
